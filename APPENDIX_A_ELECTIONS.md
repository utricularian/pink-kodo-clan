# Appendix A: Elder Elections and Vacancies

This appendix supplements the Constitution of the Pink Kodo Clan. If it conflicts with the Constitution, the Constitution controls.

## A1. Regular Elder election schedule

1. An elected Elder serves a six-month term.
2. The seven-day voting period begins seven days before the incumbent’s term ends. The winning candidate takes office immediately when the seven-day ballot closes.
3. The nomination period opens 21 days before voting begins and remains open for 21 full days. It therefore closes when voting begins.
4. Staggering the elected Elder seats is encouraged to reduce election fatigue, but it is not required. Elections may overlap.

## A2. Nomination and candidacy

1. Any member other than a Pink Horn may be nominated for an elected Elder seat.
2. A candidate must be nominated by another non-Pink Horn member. Self-nomination is not allowed.
3. A nomination becomes valid after two additional non-Pink Horn members concur. The nominator, two concurring members, and candidate must be distinct Discord users. The candidate cannot concur with their own nomination.
4. The nominee must accept the nomination. A nominee may refuse or withdraw at any time before the ballot closes.
5. Incumbents may run again but must be nominated and meet the same requirements as every other candidate.
6. Nomination and concurrence are recorded by Discord user, not character.

## A3. Election ballot and result

1. All members other than Pink Horns may vote. The eligible roster is the Discord role roster when voting opens, and each Discord user may cast one ballot.
2. Election votes are public and named. There is no minimum turnout or approval threshold.
3. The candidate receiving the most votes wins, including a candidate who receives one vote while all other candidates receive none.
4. If candidates tie for the most votes, the Anchor chooses the winner. This choice is final; there is no runoff.
5. If there are no candidates or no ballots, there is no clear winner. If the Anchor refuses or is unable to break a tie, there is no clear winner. Any no-clear-winner result automatically triggers the Elder-only dissolution vote under Section 6.5 of the Constitution.

## A4. Snap elections

Resignation, successful inactivity replacement, a successful Winds of Change vote, and any other Council vacancy requiring an elected Elder trigger a snap election. A snap election uses the same 21-day nomination period and seven-day voting period as a regular election. The winner begins a fresh six-month term when the vote closes.

## A5. Inactivity replacement

An Elder who has not logged in on any character for 21 consecutive days may be replaced by Administrative Law. Inactivity alone does not vacate the seat automatically. The affected Elder is not eligible to vote on their replacement. The motion must receive at least two-thirds of all filled Elder seats in explicit yes votes. If passed, a snap election begins under A4. A planned absence of three weeks or longer is a strong reason for the Elder to resign voluntarily instead.

## A6. Anchor succession

When there is no Anchor, the serving Elders select one of their number by a recorded Elder-only vote. The selection requires the Administrative Law threshold of two-thirds of all filled Elder seats in explicit yes votes. If a snap election is required to fill an Elder vacancy at the same time, the Elders may select the Anchor from among the resulting Council. The Council must keep to the authorized number of Elder seats.
