# Appendix B: Misconduct, Impeachment, and Sentencing

This appendix supplements the Constitution of the Pink Kodo Clan. Its purpose is to let members report misconduct, ensure the Elders investigate, and protect both the person reporting and the accused through private deliberation.

## B1. Reporting and investigation

1. Any Guild member may report misconduct to any Elder. Reports may concern an Elder, Kodo Rider, Herd Member, Pink Horn, or conduct between members. An Elder’s presence at the incident is not required.
2. The seven-day investigation period begins when the receiving Elder confirms receipt of the accusation. That Elder must take it seriously and involve the other Elders who are eligible to investigate.
3. The non-accused Elders must investigate the allegation during the seven-day period. Investigation details remain private to protect the person reporting and the accused.
4. An Elder who is accused may not vote on Administrative Laws during the investigation, direct or participate in the investigation, or participate in deciding the outcome. The accused Elder may privately present a defense or evidence to the other Elders.
5. The investigation is not a public hearing. The Elders may speak with relevant parties and consider information needed to assess the accusation.

## B2. Impeachment Vote

1. At the end of the seven-day investigation period, the non-accused Elders open a private, binary Impeachment Vote lasting seven full days.
2. The vote asks whether the Elders find that misconduct occurred and needs to be addressed.
3. Every non-accused Elder is eligible to vote. An eligible Elder who explicitly abstains or fails to cast a ballot by the deadline is counted as voting yes. An eligible Elder must explicitly vote no to block impeachment.
4. The vote passes if no eligible Elder casts a no vote. The identity of each vote and the deliberation remain private.
5. If the vote does not pass, the case closes without a sentence under this process.
6. If the vote passes, the Sentencing Vote opens immediately.

## B3. Sentencing Vote

1. The Sentencing Vote is private and offers four options: **Private Warning**, **Public Statement**, **Demotion**, and **Banishment**.
2. All non-accused Elders may vote. The accused does not vote.
3. The option with the most votes is selected. If options tie, the Anchor chooses among the tied options unless the Anchor is accused, cannot make the choice, or abstains. In those cases, the most severe tied option is selected. Severity is ordered: Private Warning, Public Statement, Demotion, Banishment.
4. Sentencing ballots last seven full days. Abstention is permitted and does not count as a vote for a sentencing option.

## B4. Sentences

- **Private Warning:** The Elders deliver a private reprimand to the person reporting and the accused, including what the Guild is doing to correct the wrong.
- **Public Statement:** Includes the Private Warning and a public statement describing the accusation and corrective actions. The statement should not disclose unnecessary personal details.
- **Demotion:** The accused is moved to a lower Guild or Discord rank where a lower rank exists. If the accused is an Elder, demotion removes them from the Council and moves them to Kodo Rider; a snap election fills the vacant Elder seat. Demotion and its outcome are public.
- **Banishment:** The accused is immediately removed from the in-game Guild and Discord. Banishment is public.

The Impeachment and Sentencing processes apply whether the accused is an Elder or another Guild member. When a non-Elder is accused, all Elders are non-accused unless an Elder is otherwise disqualified from the matter.
