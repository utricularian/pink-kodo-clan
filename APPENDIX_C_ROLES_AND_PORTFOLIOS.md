# Appendix C: Ranks and Portfolios

This appendix describes the initial Guild rank and Portfolio structure. Portfolio responsibilities may be adjusted by Administrative Law. Any change must remain consistent with the Constitution.

## C1. Rank summary

| Rank | Invite authority | Edict vote | Elder election participation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Elder | As assigned through Guild permissions | Yes | May vote and may run if the person holds an elected seat or is eligible for candidacy |
| Kodo Rider | May invite without prior authorization | Yes | May nominate, concur, vote, and run for an elected Elder seat |
| Herd Member | No invite authority | Yes | May nominate, concur, vote, and run for an elected Elder seat |
| Pink Horn | No invite authority | No | Not eligible to nominate, concur, vote, or run |

The Discord roles corresponding to these ranks determine eligibility for governance votes. Each real person is represented by one Discord user for voting, regardless of the number of in-game characters.

## C2. Pink Horn welcome period

A new member begins as a Pink Horn. After seven full days in the Guild, they automatically become a Herd Member. The welcome period is intended to give the Guild time to identify serious conduct concerns before granting voting and nomination privileges.

## C3. Initial Portfolios

### Administrator

The Administrator maintains the governance machinery: prepares and opens ballots, publishes passed laws and vote records, maintains the official Constitution and appendix copies, and records election dates and outcomes. The Administrator does not gain extra votes or authority by holding the Portfolio.

### Recruiter

The Recruiter directs whether the Guild is recruiting, what kinds of players it seeks, and how recruitment is coordinated. Kodo Riders retain their rank-based ability to invite without prior authorization. The Recruiter should communicate recruitment policies adopted by Administrative Law and manage the Pink Horn welcome process.

## C4. Portfolio administration

Portfolios are assigned or changed only by Administrative Law. The Council may assign more than one Portfolio to an Elder, change a Portfolio holder, or create additional Portfolios. Portfolio authority covers the assigned work and does not permit a holder to override a Guild law, the Constitution, or a Guild-wide Edict.
