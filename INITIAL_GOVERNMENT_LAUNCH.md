# Initial Government Launch Plan

This is a one-time transition document, separate from the Constitution. It records how the Pink Kodo Clan plans to start the council government. It is not a standing election rule.

## Initial Council

The Council begins with three Elder seats:

- One Anchor Elder, selected for the initial launch.
- One preselected elected Elder.
- One vacant elected Elder seat to be filled through the election process in Appendix A.

## Initial shortened term

The preselected elected Elder serves an initial shortened term of three months. At the end of that term, the seat returns to the ordinary six-month tenure and election schedule in the Constitution and Appendix A.

The initial Anchor and preselected Elder will be identified when the Guild adopts this launch plan. The adoption process and any transition steps are to be agreed by the current Guild Master and the founding Elders; they are intentionally not made part of the permanent Constitution.
